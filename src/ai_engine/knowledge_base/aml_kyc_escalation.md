# AML/KYC Escalation & SAR Filing Guidance
## Document ID: SOP-AML-2026-05
## Regulatory Reference: FIU-IND / FinCEN AML Regulations

### Section 1: Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) Escalation Triggers
1. **Mandatory Reporting Criteria**:
   - Known or suspected criminal activity involving funds exceeding ₹1,000,000 INR (or $5,000 USD).
   - Transactions designed to evade BSA / PMLA reporting requirements (structuring).
   - Funds derived from illegal activities, ransomware, or terrorism financing.
   - Transactions lacking any demonstrable commercial or lawful purpose.
2. **Investigation Documentation Standard**:
   Every case escalated for potential SAR filing must maintain a verifiable dossier including:
   - Clear chronology of events and transaction timeline.
   - Objective factual findings distinguished from subjective inference.
   - Corroborated evidence items (IP logs, device fingerprints, account balances).
   - Documented investigator decision and supervisor approval signature.

### Section 2: Human Sign-Off Requirement
1. Under no circumstances may an AI system or software daemon automatically generate, sign, or submit a regulatory SAR or STR report to authorities.
2. The AI platform functions strictly as an investigatory copilot to compile evidence, draft objective narratives, and calculate risk probabilities.
3. Final filing authority rests solely with the designated Compliance Officer or BSA Officer.
