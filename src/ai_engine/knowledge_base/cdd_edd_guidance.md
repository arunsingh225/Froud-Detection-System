# Customer Due Diligence (CDD) & Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) Guidance
## Document ID: SOP-CDD-2026-03
## Scope: Regulatory Compliance & Risk Stratification

### Section 1: Baseline Due Diligence
1. **Customer Risk Profiling**: Every individual or corporate customer profile maintains a calculated Risk Score (0–100) and assigned Risk Tier (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
2. **Behavioral Baseline**: Automated transaction monitoring systems calculate dynamic rolling 30-day volume and baseline average transaction amounts. Discrepancies exceeding 400% of baseline require supervisory sign-off.
3. **Periodic Review Schedules**:
   - Critical Risk accounts: Quarterly review.
   - High Risk accounts: Semi-annual review.
   - Medium Risk accounts: Annual review.
   - Low Risk accounts: Biennial review.

### Section 2: Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) Triggers
Enhanced Due Diligence is mandatory whenever:
- A transaction exceeds ₹500,000 INR (or $10,000 USD) where the risk tier is High or Critical.
- The customer is a Politically Exposed Person (PEP) or associated with high-risk jurisdictions.
- Machine learning risk score exceeds the operational decision threshold (> 80.00%).
- Multi-factor authentication was bypassed or flagged with anomaly warnings.

### Section 3: EDD Verification Requirements
1. **Proof of Source of Funds**: Verified bank statement, salary slip, or auditable business invoice.
2. **Out-of-Band Callback Verification**: Direct telephone or secure biometric verification with the registered account owner before release of flagged funds.
3. **Beneficiary Substantiation**: Documented commercial or personal relationship between payer and beneficiary entity.
